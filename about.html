About me

A Medical Radiography student who found a second passion in technology and decided to take it seriously.

My story

I'm studying Medical Radiography, a field that rewards care, accuracy, and clear communication with people who are often anxious. Those habits shape how I work in everything else I do.

Alongside my studies, my curiosity about how websites and apps work grew into a real interest in technology. I started learning frontend development by building things, and I haven't stopped since.

So far I've learned HTML, CSS, and JavaScript by making projects: a browser extensions manager, a bag store, an image gallery, a music player, and a calculator. Each one taught me something new about layout, responsiveness, or DOM manipulation. I'm still learning, and I'm happy to say so.

I'm also drawn to IT support and customer-focused work. I enjoy helping people solve problems, explaining things clearly, and keeping communication organised through email and support tools.
